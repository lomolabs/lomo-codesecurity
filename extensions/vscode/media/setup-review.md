# Start with the evidence

Use **CodeSecurity Findings**, **Problems**, a hover or a Quick Fix to reach a finding.

**Open Finding in Web** shows its severity, scanner, evidence, lifecycle and gate effect. **Open Source** navigates to its validated local location. Snippet notices explain truncated or unavailable context.

After editing, save and rescan. Old evidence becomes stale; it does not silently describe the new file.

A clean scan may have no findings to review. The checklist does not invent a finding just to mark this step complete.
